Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026
This policy explains what Herculean stores about you and why.

1. What we store

Your email, name, a protected form of your password that can’t be read, your optional profile photo, your settings, the routines, exercises and groups you set up, and the workouts and sets you log.

2. Why

Only to run the app: to sign you in, show your workouts on every device, and calculate your history and records.

3. What we don’t do

No ads, no selling your data, no sharing with other companies except the hosting provider that stores it and the email service that sends our emails.

4. Where it lives

On servers in the European Union, protected in transit and at rest.

5. Your rights

You can see, correct and delete your data. Deleting your account in Settings removes everything permanently after 14 days.

6. Contact

Privacy questions: hello@minimalist.hr.
